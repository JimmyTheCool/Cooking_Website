How to make Hiroshima okonomiyaki:
At edge of heat:
1. 80% large ladle of batter, thin circle size of hand
Move to centre of heat:
2. Large handful of shredded cabbage (approx 150g)
3. Small ladle of tempura crisp
4. Small amount of protein (e.g. squid tempura/chicken)
5. Pinch of chopped spring onion
6. Half handful of beansprouts
7. ?Sprinkle fish powder
8. 3 strips of overlapping pork belly
9. 20% large ladle of batter - drizzle over pork (reduces shrinkage)
10. Flip okonomiyaki over - up, away & flip - to ensure lands over heat
At edge of heat:
11. Open bag of noodles - drop from high 
12. Check that pork cooked, then move current okonomiyaki away from heat 
13. Noodles steps:
a. Move into heat 
b. Pour 2 rounds of dashi 
c. Move away from heat
d. Loosen noodles with edges of spatulas
e. Shape noodles into a circle, size larger than current okonomiyaki 
f. Lift okonomiyaki and place onto noodles
14. Onto edge of heat, crack 1 egg, break yolk, spread into a circle by stirring 3 times with flat back of spatula
15. While egg is still semi wet, place okonomiyaki onto egg 
16. Flip entire okonomiyaki over
17. Move to front edge of grill in preparation to eat
18. Dress with 1 large ladle of okonomiyaki sauce, drizzle mayo as desired & sprinkle seaweed bits 
19. EAT!!


Ingredients:
Batter: (Quantity unspecified, but you'll need enough for two ladles per okonomiyaki)
Shredded Cabbage: Approximately 150g per okonomiyaki
Tempura Crisps: (Small ladle per okonomiyaki)
Protein: (Small amount per okonomiyaki, options include squid tempura or chicken)
Spring Onion: (Pinch, chopped, per okonomiyaki)
Beansprouts: (Half handful per okonomiyaki)
Fish Powder: (Sprinkle per okonomiyaki)
Pork Belly: 3 strips per okonomiyaki
Noodles: (One bag per okonomiyaki)
Dashi: (Two rounds per okonomiyaki)
Egg: 1 per okonomiyaki
Okonomiyaki Sauce: (One large ladle per okonomiyaki)
Mayonnaise: (As desired)
Seaweed Bits: (Sprinkle per okonomiyaki)
